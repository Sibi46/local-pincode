# Account, business follows and preferred areas

- Business owners use `/employer/dashboard/#my-profile` for all profile editing. Personal details, profile photo, business details, logo, banner, phone, main/preferred pincodes and any existing work/education details are edited together inside the dashboard. Saves and validation remain on the dashboard. The profile icon and Company Profile tab open the editor in place; `/profile/edit/` redirects business owners to it. Other account types retain their existing profile editor. The same stored records are used by the public business page.
- Primary headers share one template: the home logo and account icon. Profile, dashboard, sign-out, appearance and feature access are available in the account hub. Feature-specific controls and permissions are retained. This replaces primary headers across 52 module templates, including the base layout, Campus, Community, Health, Jobs, messages and business pages.
- Business pages and directory cards offer Follow/Following, with persistent counts and a private Following filter. Following is authenticated, uses POST and CSRF protection, cannot target the user's own or an inactive business, and is unique per customer/business. Repeated Follow or Unfollow requests are idempotent. Review totals remain correct when a business has multiple followers.
- Users keep one main pincode plus up to two optional preferred areas (Quest pincodes). Values must be distinct valid six-digit pincodes. Preferences stay private. Guests can choose an area without registering. The area selector offers saved-area shortcuts; exploring another area does not overwrite the main pincode or business address. Business, job, community and News Desk discovery use the selected area, with explicit filters and an all-area reset supported. Registration forms prefill the selected area.
- Existing offer-page work was left separate during implementation.

Migration: `jobs.0061_user_preferred_pincode_1_user_preferred_pincode_2_and_more` adds two blank optional fields and the business-follow table. No existing user pincode is replaced.

Validation: 113 account, registration, application, business, usability and News Desk regression tests pass. Browser checks cover guest location selection, business profile saves, preferred areas, Follow/Unfollow persistence and counters, the private Following list, and shared headers on ten main modules at mobile width. No page JavaScript errors were observed. Screenshots are saved in the workspace's `qa-report` directory.
